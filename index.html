WELCOME TO MY WEBSITE!

Here Is My Creative Statement

“I can never make it.” That's something that many first-generation students have thought about while trying to achieve dreams that their parents have never had. Being first-generation is taking up spaces that our parents dreamed of, not letting them down, and just getting things done on your own since our parents weren't taught about the things that you're studying. Feeling the imposter syndrome of taking up spaces that “weren't meant for us” or feeling bad for making familial sacrifices in order for us to pursue higher education. However, it will be worth it in the end. Endless sacrifices, our parents working to the bone just to make ends meet. Being first-generation is not only a title. It's an accomplishment and a milestone for many immigrant families who didn't get their chance for educational opportunities. Recently, there has been a rise in first-generation latinos, from various latino backgrounds, earning their degrees representing different industries. No matter how long it takes, a degree is a degree.
